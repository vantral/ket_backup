## About the corpus

The corpus includes texts from three Ket dialects. Most recordings were made in 2002–2014 during field trips by LALS, Moscow State University, and LISMYA; the collection also includes A. K. Korsakov's archival materials from 1937. Translations and linguistic annotation accompany the texts.

## Search facilities

- word forms, lemmas, and translations;
- grammatical features and glosses;
- text and dialect metadata;
- full-text display and associated multimedia.

Complex agreement and verb-series labels are explained component by component. Select a label in the catalogue below to open its note.
